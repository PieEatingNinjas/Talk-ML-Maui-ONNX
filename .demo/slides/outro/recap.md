# Recap
## Why ML?
- Some problems are too complex for rules
- Rules often break down or don’t generalize in real data
- Real patterns are buried in data and not visible to humans

## Why ML.NET?
- Stay in the .NET ecosystem and tooling you already know and love
- Use (pre-trained) ONNX models
- Works everywhere .NET runs: web, console, desktop, mobile, cloud
